# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js, explicitly selected by the user. Static export suitable for hosting; interactions and progress are browser-local.
## Users
Thai vocational diploma students with little networking background. Primary operator is the teacher presenting at the front of the classroom.
## Product Purpose
Teach week one networking and OSI using the approved OSI material (original chapters 03–10). Preserve its explanations rather than replacing them with summaries alone.
## Operating Context
Two class meetings of two and three periods. Teacher-led presentation, with student review possible on smaller screens.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Full Thai lesson reading, step-controlled OSI visualizations and selectable OSI layers. No real network tests: any terminal activity must clearly say simulation. No accounts or claimed server-side assessment. Private hosting by default.

## Product Principles
Preserve complete explanations. Make layers and packet flow visible. Let the teacher control pace. Keep examples distinct from actual device values. Use Thai that beginners can understand.
